1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BRITUME ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the BRITUME mobile application and related services. BRITUME is designed to support community, communication, entertainment, creativity, technology, and innovation.

By using BRITUME, you acknowledge this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will request that consent separately.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, BRITUME may process the following:

  • Account information: email address, username, display name, authentication details, and account identifiers.
  • Profile information: profile picture, avatar, biography, and other information you choose to provide.
  • User content: posts, comments, messages if messaging is enabled, images, videos, and other content you submit.
  • Preferences: selected themes, wallpapers, notification settings, and other app preferences.
  • Technical information: app version, device and operating-system information, diagnostic records, and security events where these are made available to our services.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting us for help.

The information actually collected depends on which features are enabled and the permissions you grant. We do not intend to collect information that a feature does not need.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users.
  • Provide profiles, social features, posts, and other requested services.
  • Save preferences and personalize the app experience.
  • Deliver security notices and notification settings you enable.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, protect, and improve BRITUME.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and violations of our rules.

4. Account Authentication and Service Providers

BRITUME may use Supabase or other configured service providers for account authentication, database services, and storage. Information sent to these providers is handled according to the services actually configured and their applicable privacy terms.

Never share your password or security PIN with another person. Use a strong, unique password and keep your device secure.

5. User Content and Sharing

If you publish content through social or community features, that content may be visible to other users according to the feature's visibility and access settings. Do not publish information you want to keep private.

Content you download or save may be stored on your device. You are responsible for ensuring you have permission to upload, share, or download content when required.

6. Photos, Media, and Device Permissions

If you choose a profile image, wallpaper, or other media, BRITUME may access the media you select through the relevant device picker or permission. Access should be limited to what is needed for the feature.

You can manage app permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

7. Notifications

If notifications are supported and enabled, BRITUME or its configured service providers may process the information needed to deliver them. You can change notification permissions in your device settings and any notification preferences provided within the app.

8. Advertising, Analytics, and Purchases

BRITUME may introduce advertising, analytics, or purchase services in the future. If these services are enabled, their providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device data, interaction events, or transaction records as described in the applicable provider policies and in any disclosures or consent requests presented to you.

Advertising, analytics, and purchase features that are not enabled are not represented by this policy as currently operating. This policy must be updated before launch or activation of services that require additional disclosures.

9. When Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary:

  • With service providers that operate app functions on our behalf.
  • With other users when you choose to publish or share content.
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid government request.
  • To investigate abuse, protect users, or safeguard the service and its rights.
  • As part of a business transfer, where lawful and appropriately protected.

We do not claim to sell your personal information. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide notices where required.

10. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accounts, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and protect the platform. Retention periods vary by data type.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can review information you provide in the app, change available preferences, manage device permissions, and contact us about privacy requests. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

12. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion through any account-deletion option available in BRITUME or by contacting us using the details below. We will explain any verification steps that are necessary.

When a deletion request is verified, we will delete or anonymize associated personal information within a reasonable period, except information we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other lawful purposes. Some information already shared with other users may remain in their possession or records.

13. Children's Privacy

BRITUME is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can assess the request and take appropriate action.

Before making BRITUME available to children or targeting children, we will assess applicable age, consent, safety, and platform requirements.

14. International Data Processing

Depending on the service providers used, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied to international transfers.

15. Changes to This Policy
